ABOUT FUELLOG

A clearer picture of what you eat.

FuelLog is a simple food and nutrition journal that helps you record meals, understand your daily calorie and protein totals, and notice patterns across the week.

It’s built to make food logging feel practical and informative—not to label foods as “good” or “bad”.

QUICK START

How to use FuelLog

Start with one entry. You can add detail and explore your history as you go.

01

Choose a day

Use the date selector in the top bar. Days with food logged are highlighted in the calendar, so you can jump back to an earlier entry.

02

Add a food or drink

Enter the food name, portion, number of servings, time eaten, calories per serving and protein per serving. Use the packaging or a reliable nutrition source for the values.

03

Review before saving

Select Review entry and check the per-serving values and calculated totals. Choose Confirm and add when they look right, or edit the details first.

04

Reuse and refine

Edit or copy a logged item, bookmark foods you often eat, or use the Recent foods list to fill the form faster next time.

MADE FOR EVERYDAY USE

Your tools, at a glance

Everything is designed to help you understand your diary without making it complicated.

Daily food log

See entries in time order, with total calories and protein for the selected day. You can edit, duplicate, bookmark or remove an entry.

Nutrition breakdown

Click the Calories logged or Protein logged card to open a pie chart showing how each food contributes to that day's total.

Weekly history

Compare days with calorie and protein trend bars, review averages across days logged, and move between previous weeks.

My Foods and recent foods

Build a personal library of foods with portions, calories and protein. Matching a saved name in Daily Log fills in those details for quicker review, and recent foods are easy to reuse.

Calendar and streaks

Green calendar markers show logged dates. The streak badge appears at three or more consecutive logging days; use it as context, not pressure.

Profile and privacy

Your profile provides broad estimates. Your diary is stored in this browser; it does not automatically sync between devices.

ⓘ

A few important things to know

Nutrition values depend on the portions and sources you enter. FuelLog is a tracking tool, not a medical service, and its targets are estimates rather than prescriptions. It does not set a calorie-deficit target for users under 18. Your profile, food log, bookmarks and settings are saved locally in this browser, so clearing site data or switching browsers/devices may remove access to that history unless you have a separate backup.

Enter nutrition values from the food packaging or a reliable source. Your profile and diary are saved in this browser on this device; they do not sync automatically between devices.
