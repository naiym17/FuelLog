About your nutrition targetsFor adults, FuelLog estimates maintenance energy and a modest planning range. For anyone under 18, it won't set a weight-loss calorie deficit because energy needs during growth should be guided individually by a qualified health professional.

FuelLog stores information locally in this browser. It does not create an online account or sync between devices. Estimates are for general information, not medical advice.
